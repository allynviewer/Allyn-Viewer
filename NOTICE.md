# Allyn Viewer

This software is not provided or supported by Linden Lab, the makers of Second Life.

Linden Lab does not support this program. Allyn Viewer support is through GitHub (https://github.com/allynviewer/Allyn-Viewer/issues and https://github.com/allynviewer/Allyn-Viewer/discussions), Discord (https://discord.gg/y6uaVeD79m), and e-mail (allynviewer@gmail.com).

Privacy policy: https://allynviewer.org/privacidade and https://github.com/allynviewer/Allyn-Viewer/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Restrained Love (RLVa) is on by default. A script in an object you wear can restrict the viewer, including detaching items, chat, inventory, and teleport. RLVa is also used with adult content. Turn it off in preferences. That change applies after a restart.

AI translation is off until you turn it on and supply your own API key.

While logged into Second Life, a presence heartbeat (avatar UUID and viewer version) is sent about once a minute. There is no preference to turn it off. It is not sent on other grids.

Crash reports default to Always send. Choose Never send in Preferences, General, to stop them.

Nothing is downloaded or installed as an update until you accept.

Animation override (AO) is off by default. Invisible avatar is off by default.
